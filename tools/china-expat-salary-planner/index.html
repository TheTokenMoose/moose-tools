Why this tool exists

This calculator helps expats understand their expected take-home income in China. It uses available tax rules, mathematical modelling, and user-provided information to provide an estimate. It is designed to improve transparency and help users make informed decisions.

This is not a payroll system and does not replace HR departments, employers, government calculations, or professional tax advice.

Chinese Tax Explained Simply

1. China uses cumulative taxation

For tax residents, income earned since January is tracked cumulatively. This means someone starting in August may have a different tax calculation compared with someone who started in January.

2. The basic idea

Gross Income ↓ Monthly deduction ↓ Taxable Income ↓ Tax Brackets ↓ Tax Already Paid Removed ↓ Final Tax Due

3. Why your tax changes during the year

Your monthly tax amount may increase or decrease because the system recalculates your position throughout the calendar year.

About China Expat Salary Planner

This tool exists to help expats understand their financial position when working in China.

Employment contracts can be difficult to interpret. Salary figures may not clearly explain taxes, deductions, contract timing, or actual take-home income.

The goal is clarity, not false precision.
