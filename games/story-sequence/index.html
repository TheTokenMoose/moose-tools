Story Sequence Board

Sort the pictures. Sort the sentences. Retell classic tales.

How to play
  1. Choose a story (all are public-domain classics / fables).
  2. Panel stage — put the story scenes in order.
  3. Sentence stage — put the sentences in order.
  4. Earn stars, then try another tale.

Public-domain texts adapted for classroom sequencing. Illustrations are simple scene cards, not copies of any modern book art.
